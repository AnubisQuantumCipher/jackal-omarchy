# Community Conduct

This project welcomes technical disagreement, careful criticism, and evidence
that a claim or implementation is wrong. Discussion must remain professional.

Contributors are expected to:

- distinguish observations, inferences, estimates, and verified artifacts;
- preserve refusal reasons, non-claims, and uncertainty boundaries;
- critique code and claims without harassment or personal attacks;
- avoid publishing credentials, private data, or exploit details in public;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- respect licenses, authorship, and attribution;
- accept that consequential claims require stronger review than presentation
  or convenience features.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when
behavior threatens contributors, users, security, or the integrity of the
project's evidence record. Security concerns should follow `SECURITY.md` rather
than being posted publicly.
